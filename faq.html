Website Project Questions

What business owners usually ask before starting.

Question

What kind of websites do you build?

I build business-focused websites for small businesses that need a stronger online presence, clearer messaging, and an easier way for customers to take the next step. That can include service websites, redesigns, landing pages, portfolio-style business sites, and websites built around quote requests or contact forms, including Houston web design for small businesses, cleaning business website design, fencing company website design, concrete company website design, and roofing company website design.

Question

Do you build custom websites or use templates?

My goal is to build websites around the business, not force every business into the same layout. I can use proven structure and design patterns, but the sections, wording, flow, and calls to action should match the business, the services, and the customers the website is trying to reach. For businesses that need a simpler foundation or modern deployment workflow, I also build Vercel websites, static business websites, GitHub Pages websites, and custom HTML/CSS websites.

Question

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. If your current website feels outdated, confusing, hard to trust, or weak on mobile, a website redesign can clean up the layout, improve the message, strengthen the calls to action, and make the site easier for visitors to understand and use.

Question

What do I need before starting a website project?

The main things I need are your business name, services, service areas, contact information, logo or brand assets if you have them, photos if available, and a clear idea of what you want the website to help with. If you do not have everything ready, I can still help organize the structure and identify what is missing.

Question

Can you help with SEO?

Yes. I focus on practical SEO structure: clear page titles, descriptions, headings, service wording, internal links, local or B2B keyword structure, and content clarity. I do not guarantee rankings, but I build the website in a way that helps search engines and customers better understand what the business offers.

Question

Do you offer monthly website care?

Yes. Monthly website care can include hosting support, security-minded checks, continuous maintenance, SEO improvements, and content or design edits. This is for businesses that want their website maintained, updated, and improved over time instead of letting it sit untouched after launch.

Question

Can you handle hosting and domain setup?

I can help with hosting setup, domain and DNS guidance, SSL/HTTPS review, and making sure the website has a stable place to live online. If a business already has hosting or a domain, I can review what is there and help point it in the right direction, including whether Vercel website development fits the project.

Question

Can you add contact forms or quote request forms?

Yes. I can set up contact forms, quote request forms, and lead capture sections that make it easier for visitors to reach out. The goal is to collect the right information without making the form feel confusing or overwhelming.

Question

How long does a website project take?

The timeline depends on the size of the website, the amount of content needed, and how quickly information is provided. A smaller website can move faster, while a larger redesign or multi-page site needs more planning, structure, and review. The focus is to build it correctly instead of rushing through details that matter.

Question

How do I know which service I need?

If you are not sure, start by sending details about your business, your current website if you have one, and what you want the site to improve. I can help you figure out whether you need a new website, a redesign, updates, SEO structure, automation, or monthly care.
